They Promised Automatic Posting. The 2023 Teachers Are Still Waiting.

I still remember the speeches. When they were in opposition, they stood on platforms and shouted about how teachers would never again sit at home after qualifying. They said recruitment would be automatic. No more endless waiting. No more broken dreams. They made it sound so simple and so urgent.

Now they are in power, and the same young men and women who believed those words are still sitting at home.

The 2023 batch has become a quiet tragedy. These are people who studied, wrote exams, passed, and then waited. Months turned into a year. A year is turning into something longer. Some have started small businesses just to survive. Others are teaching in private schools for little money. Many just stay home, refreshing their phones every day, hoping the next announcement will finally carry their names.

And what do we hear from the government? They say the teachers have been posted. They say the process is ongoing. They say everything is under control.

But the teachers know the truth. Their families know the truth. The communities that need teachers know the truth. Saying people have been posted when they are still at home is not just a delay — it is a public insult.

This is not about one department making a mistake. This is about a promise that was used to win support, then quietly abandoned once power was secured. When you tell people “we will post you automatically” and then leave them waiting indefinitely, you are not managing a system. You are managing people’s hope, and you are managing it poorly.

Every day a qualified teacher sits idle, a classroom somewhere is short of a teacher. Children lose learning time. Parents get frustrated. The teacher loses years of their working life. The damage spreads quietly but deeply.

I am not asking for miracles. I am asking for honesty and urgency. Publish the real list. Clear the backlog. Stop pretending the problem has been solved when thousands of trained teachers are still waiting for their letters.

The 2023 batch did their part. They prepared. They wrote the exams. They passed. It is time for the government to do theirs.

If this is how promises made in opposition are treated once power is won, then we should all pay closer attention the next time the same voices climb onto the podium and make new ones.
