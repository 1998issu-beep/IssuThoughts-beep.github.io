They Promised Automatic Posting. The 2023 Teachers Are Still Waiting.

August 28, 2026

There is a particular kind of frustration that comes from being told to wait for something that was supposed to happen automatically.

For teachers from the 2023 recruitment cycle, the promise of automatic posting created an expectation: that after meeting the necessary requirements, the next step would not require another long struggle.

The promise and the waiting

Waiting becomes harder when there is little clarity about when the waiting will end. People make plans around opportunities, salaries and careers, and uncertainty has a cost.

Beyond numbers

Behind every delayed posting is a person trying to build a life. The issue is therefore not merely administrative. It is also about dignity, time and the ability to plan for the future.

What clarity would mean

Even where a process takes time, clear communication matters. People deserve to know what stage a process has reached, what remains outstanding and what they should reasonably expect next.

The teachers are not asking for a miracle. They are asking for clarity and for the promise made to them to be treated seriously.
